WakeUs Terms of Use

Effective 2026-10-08

1. The service

WakeUs lets you create groups, invite people by nickname and share alarms. A group alarm rings on every member's own iPhone and only stops for good when everyone has turned it off.

2. Important: alarms are not guaranteed

Your alarm depends on your device, its settings, battery, iOS updates and permissions. WakeUs is provided "as is" and we can't guarantee it will wake you. Do not rely on WakeUs alone for anything critical such as flights, exams, work shifts or medication — always set a backup.

3. Your account

You must be at least 13 (14 in Korea). Keep your account to yourself. Your nickname must not impersonate anyone or contain offensive content.

4. Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abuse

By using WakeUs you agree not to post objectionable content — including harassment, hate speech, sexual content, threats or spam — in nicknames, group names, alarm titles or reports, and not to abuse invites or nudges. We filter objectionable words automatically. You can report and block anyone in the app. We review reports within 24 hours and remove offending content and remove or ban the users responsible.

5. Price

WakeUs is free. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads. Features may change over time.

6. Ending

You can delete your account at any time. We may suspend accounts that break these terms. We may end the service with reasonable notice in the app.

8. Changes
