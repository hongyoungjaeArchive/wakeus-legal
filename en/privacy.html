WakeUs Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-01

What we collect

  • Your Apple sign-in identifier (we never see your Apple password; you may hide your email).
  • The name and WakeUs ID you choose.
  • Alarms you create or join: title, time, repeat days, time zone and members.
  • Wake-up records: when you confirmed you were up, and nudges sent or received.
  • Friends, blocks and reports you make.
  • A push notification token for your device, and your WakeUs+ purchase status.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or microphone, and we don't use third-party analytics or ads.

Why

To run group alarms, show friends who is awake, deliver nudges, show your history, keep the service safe (blocks and reports) and restore purchases.

Who can see it

  • Friends in the same alarm see your name, ID and wake-up status for that alarm.
  • Anyone signed in can find you by your exact WakeUs ID.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Supabase (database and servers, Tokyo region), Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications, App Store purchases).

We never sell your data.

How long

Until you delete your account. Reports may be kept for safety reviews without your profile attached.

Your choices

Delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, alarms, friends and history and revokes the Apple sign-in link. You can turn off notifications in iPhone Settings. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK, California or Japan), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data — contact us.

Children

WakeUs is not intended for children under 13.
