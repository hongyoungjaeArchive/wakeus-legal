WakeUs Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-02

What we collect

  • Your Sign in with Apple identifier, and the email address Apple shares with us (this can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"). We never see your Apple password.
  • Your nickname. It is unique and public inside WakeUs: anyone signed in can type your exact nickname to invite you to a group.
  • Groups you create or join: group name, members, who invited whom.
  • Alarms in your groups: title, time, repeat days, time zone and sound settings (sound settings stay on your phone).
  • Wake-up records: when you confirmed you were up, and nudges sent or received.
  • Your notification inbox (invites, alarm changes, members joining or leaving).
  • Reports and blocks you make, including the text you write in a report.
  • Abuse-prevention records: when you create groups and send invites (used only for rate limits).
  • A push notification token for your device, and your WakeUs+ purchase status.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or microphone, and we don't use third-party analytics, ads or tracking.

Why

To run group alarms, show group members who is awake, deliver invites and nudges, show your history, keep the community safe (filters, reports, blocks, rate limits) and restore purchases.

Who can see it

  • Members of your groups see your nickname, membership and wake-up status in those groups.
  • People you invite, or who invite you, see your nickname and the group name.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Supabase (database and servers, Tokyo, Japan) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications, App Store purchases).

We never sell your data.

How long

Until you delete your account. Reports may be kept for safety reviews without your profile attached. Abuse-prevention records are kept for up to 30 days.

Your choices

Delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, the groups you host (and their alarms), your alarms and history, removes you from other people's groups, and revokes the Sign in with Apple link. You can turn off notifications in iPhone Settings and block anyone in the app. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK, California, Japan or Korea) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data — contact us.

Children

WakeUs is not intended for children under 13.
